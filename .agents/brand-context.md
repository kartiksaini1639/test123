# Brand Context

> Scrollr Labs is Kartik's own India-based agency and hasn't launched yet: no clients, case studies or testimonials so far. Items marked _(assumed)_ or TBD still need confirming.

## Brand
- **Name**: Scrollr Labs
- **Category**: Digital agency: web design, SEO and digital marketing
- **Description**: Scrollr Labs builds websites, gets them found on Google and runs the marketing that turns visitors into customers for small and medium businesses.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch (founder-led personal agency, not yet on the market)
- **Website**: https://scrollrlabs.in (planned, not live yet)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Owners and managers of small and medium businesses (SMBs) in India and the US: local service businesses, retailers, clinics, restaurants, consultants and growing startups that need a stronger online presence but have no in-house marketing team.
- **Key Problem**: Their website is outdated, slow or missing, they don't show up when customers search, and they've spent money on marketing without seeing clear results. They lack the time and expertise to fix it themselves and are wary of agencies that overpromise.
- **Their Language**: "I need more customers / more leads", "we're not on Google", "our website looks old", "is it worth the money?", "I don't understand SEO", "I want enquiries on WhatsApp", "affordable", "one team for everything" _(assumed; replace with real phrases from the first prospect conversations)_

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: A founder-led agency where clients work directly with the person running their project, backed by a network of trusted freelance specialists so it can scale to bigger jobs without big-agency overhead. One partner for the full online growth stack (design, SEO and marketing) built specifically for SMB budgets and timelines, instead of juggling a freelancer for the site, another for SEO and an ad agency on top. Plain-language reporting tied to leads and sales, not vanity metrics.
- **Competitors**: Solo freelancers on Fiverr/Upwork and local web designers (some may also become delivery partners), DIY builders (Wix, Shopify, WordPress templates), and large full-service digital agencies _(confirm named local competitors)_
- **Market Position**: Value: agency-quality work at SMB-friendly pricing. India-based delivery keeps prices competitive for US clients.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Approachable, sharp, results-driven, honest, hungry
- **Tone**: Casual but professional; confident and clear, lightly playful, never jargon-heavy

- **Core Values**: Results over vanity metrics; transparency in pricing and reporting; speed without cutting corners; small business first; plain language
- **Mission**: Give small and medium businesses the online presence and steady flow of customers that used to be reserved for big brands with big budgets. _(working draft)_
