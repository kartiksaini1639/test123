# Ghost Town — Shot List (image + animation prompts)

16 shots, ~2:18 total, 16:9 (1920×1080). Each shot has two steps:

1. **Make the still image.** Use any image generator, with the **Image prompt** plus the **Style block** and **Negative prompt** below.
2. **Animate the still.** Upload the image to any image-to-video tool, then paste the **Animation prompt**.

Times are estimates at a documentary pace (~150 words per minute, plus 0.5s of breathing room). Record the voiceover first, then cut each clip to match the real length.

---

## Rules for every shot

### Style block
Paste this at the end of every image prompt. It's already included in each prompt below, so you can copy whole prompts as they are.

> Editorial mixed-media paper collage style: illustrated paper cutouts with thick white sticker borders and soft drop shadows, layered in shallow depth on textured aged-cream paper. Halftone dot accents, washi tape strips at corners, one or two flat geometric shapes in muted gold (#D4A017). Limited palette: aged cream, charcoal ink, deep indigo (#1E2340), muted gold (#D4A017). Bold condensed editorial typography, generous margins, clear focal hierarchy, flat graphic lighting, visible print-media grain. 16:9 composition.

### Negative prompt
Use this as the negative prompt for every image, or add "Avoid:" in front of it if your tool has no negative field.

> photorealistic, 3D render, CGI, glossy lighting, lens flare, gradient mesh, anime, cartoon mascot, watercolor wash, recognizable celebrity face, realistic human face, detailed facial features, extra fingers, warped text, misspelled text, watermark, logo, signature, cluttered composition, neon colors, more than one accent color

### Animation rules
Add this to the end of every animation prompt (it's already included below):

> Motion graphics style, not live footage. All elements remain flat paper cutouts with white borders. Slow, steady 2D camera only. No camera shake, no handheld feel, no 3D rotation, no orbiting, no perspective change. No face animation, no lip movement. Text stays perfectly sharp and unchanged. No morphing, no new objects appearing, no style or colour change. Smooth ease-in and ease-out.

### Recurring characters (keep these consistent)
- **"The Man":** a faceless charcoal-black paper silhouette of a man in a loose hoodie, no facial features, with a thin white sticker border. Never draw a recognisable face.
- **"The Record":** a 1960s seven-inch vinyl single with a faded cream paper label and a small gold star on the label.
- **"The Street":** an abandoned Old West ghost-town main street. Wooden storefronts with broken windows, a leaning water tower, distant desert mesas, indigo dusk sky.

**Tip for consistency:** after you make Shot 1, give it to your image tool as a style or reference image for every later shot, if the tool supports that. If it has a seed setting, keep the same seed.
