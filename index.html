What our customers say

Questions we hear often

How much roof space do I need?

Roughly 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free roof per kW. A typical 3 kW home system needs about 300 square feet.

Will solar work during a power cut?

A standard on-grid system shuts down during an outage for the safety of line workers. If you need backup, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

What is net metering?

A two-way meter records the power you draw from the grid and the surplus you export. You are billed only for the difference.

Is a rented home or a flat eligible?

Individual subsidy needs the electricity connection and roof rights in your name. Flat owners usually go solar through their housing society instead.

How long do panels last, and what maintenance is needed?

Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty and the inverter usually 5 to 10 years. Rinse the panels with plain water every two to four weeks.

Can I pay in EMIs?

Yes. We help you apply for solar loans with partner banks, including the low-interest loans offered under PM Surya Ghar.
