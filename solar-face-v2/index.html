How it works in India

From application to net-metering, Solar Face manages the complete solar journey for you.

  1. Consultation and site survey

    We check your roof, shading and past bills to size the right system.

  2. Subsidy and DISCOM approvals

    We register you on the PM Surya Ghar portal and apply for net metering.

  3. Installation

    Structure, panels and inverter installed by our team, usually within days.

  4. Net metering and commissioning

    The net meter is fitted, the system goes live and your subsidy is claimed.

  5. Save from day one

    Track generation on your phone with our support on call.
